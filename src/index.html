          About OpenWave

          OpenWave v… — a free and open-source music player that streams audio from YouTube.

          Built with Electron and yt-dlp. No accounts, no ads, no telemetry — your playlists, likes and history never leave this computer.

          Distributed under the MIT license. If tracks stop playing, update yt-dlp: sudo yt-dlp -U

          ⚠️ Streaming from YouTube violates YouTube's Terms of Service. Personal use is a well-established gray area (same as other FOSS players) — don't sell or app-store this app.
